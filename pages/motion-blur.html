Ray tracing motion blur test

Feature wgpu-ray-tracing-motion-blur (Vulkan, NVIDIA). Two quads move from left to right between time 0 and 1: the lower one by its TLAS instance's motion transform, the upper one by its BLAS's motion vertices. traceRayMotion traces at times 0, 0.5 and 1 (left to right), and hit shaders read @builtin(ray_time). Results are also printed as MOTION ... lines.
