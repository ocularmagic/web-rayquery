Cooperative matrix test

Feature wgpu-cooperative-matrix: one subgroup multiplies matrices on the tensor cores with WGSL coopMultiplyAdd, C = A·B + C, accumulating in f32. Element types: f16 (16×16×16), and on Vulkan with VK_KHR_shader_bfloat16 / VK_EXT_shader_float8 the prototype's bf16 (16×16×16) and f8e4m3 / f8e5m2 (16×16×32). The bf16 and float8 matrices are loaded from u32 arrays (the bits are reinterpreted). Small integers keep every product exact, so the results must equal the CPU's. Results are also printed as COOPMAT ... lines.
