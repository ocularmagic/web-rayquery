Shader execution reordering test

A deliberately incoherent workload: 1024×1024 rays aimed at random spots on 64 squares, each square sent to one of 8 hit groups with its own expensive closest-hit shader. Without reordering, neighbouring invocations run different hit shaders; traceRayReordered lets the GPU regroup them first. Both must produce the same image; the GPU time of each is measured with timestamps. Results are also printed as SER ... lines.
